# Aethelgrad Concept-Art Production Brief

## Shared visual language

Original anime-fantasy survival RPG concept art for Aethelgrad: Wild Horizons — Crafting. Bright, stylized 3D-inspired illustration, clean readable silhouettes, practical adventure clothing, expressive faces, controlled saturation, soft atmospheric lighting, painterly material detail, warm ember-orange practical lights, deep teal and moss-green accents, violet wisteria flowers, weathered timber and stone, and clear gameplay-oriented composition. The mood is hopeful frontier survival: beautiful, lived-in, and slightly mysterious, never grimdark.

All characters, creatures, symbols, architecture, weapons, and environments must be original for Aethelgrad. Do not imitate or reproduce any named game, anime, logo, screenshot, recognizable character, proprietary map, or existing franchise design.

## Required assets

1. **Vision key art:** Aurora and Emberling overlooking the Wisteria frontier camp at dusk, Heartfire glowing, forest and ruins beyond, cinematic landscape composition, no text.
2. **Aurora character sheet:** Original anime-fantasy woman, warm medium skin, dark teal-black swept hair, cream-and-teal practical adventurer outfit, warm brass details, short sword and utility satchel; front/side/back/three-quarter views and expression studies, neutral studio background.
3. **Emberling companion sheet:** Small original foxlike aether creature, charcoal-to-copper fur, ember markings and glowing tail tip, multiple expressions and reaction poses, neutral studio background.
4. **Wisteria Forest environment sheet:** Frontier camp, meadow, riverbend, Rootway ruins, Mossfang cave, Warden grove; five labeled-feeling vignettes but with no readable text, coherent architectural language.
5. **Forest Warden boss sheet:** Original bark-and-moss guardian with branch-antler silhouette, green-gold aether core, phase variations, attack silhouettes, weak-point callout areas, scale comparison with Aurora; no text.
6. **Props and crafting sheet:** Heartfire, workbench, storage box, bedroll, lantern, resource nodes, cooking station, basic build pieces; orthographic-like concept presentation, no text.
7. **Mobile HUD/login visual mockup:** Landscape phone game UI with translucent dark panels, warm gold accents, left joystick, right combat controls, top-left mini-map, top-center quest, bottom-center health/stamina, and a clear Google sign-in screen; minimal legible labels only: AETHELGRAD, SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE, ONLINE READY.
8. **Lighting and weather board:** Same forest location shown in day, afternoon, evening, night, clear, rain, and thunderstorm moods; no text.

## Acceptance criteria

The set must look like one game: repeated Aurora anchors, repeated palette, consistent environment architecture, readable scale, original silhouettes, no franchise resemblance, no unnecessary typography, and strong enough hierarchy for a production art team to use as a visual target.
