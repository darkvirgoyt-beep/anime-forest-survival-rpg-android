This folder contains the first coherent visual target for **Aethelgard: Wild Horizons — Crafting**. The images are original concept references for pre-production and should be treated as art-direction material until they pass the production asset pipeline described in `PREPRODUCTION_BIBLE.md`.

## Production handoff order

The art team should use the key art to align composition and palette, then lock Aurora and Emberling identity anchors, then block the forest region and props, then develop the Forest Warden and encounter readability, and finally translate the visual direction into HUD, lighting, VFX, materials, animation, LOD, and device-tier budgets.

Generated images are concept references, not final game assets. Final assets must be original or compatible licensed content and require source tracking, modeling or illustration source files, material definitions, rigging and animation plans where applicable, optimization targets, and device validation.
