# Aethelgrad Approved Art Direction

## Status

**Approved visual direction:** use `assets/aethelgard_high_end_visual_target.jpg` as the canonical mood, composition, material, lighting, and world-presentation reference for Aethelgrad: Wild Horizons – Crafting.

![Approved Aethelgrad visual direction](../assets/aethelgard_high_end_visual_target.jpg)

## Visual statement

Aethelgrad is a cinematic fantasy survival RPG with a premium anime heroine, handcrafted frontier villages, readable rivers and waterfalls, layered mountain silhouettes, and strong biome contrast. The scene should feel like a high-end Blender and Unreal production while remaining readable on Android through scalable materials, LODs, effects, and frame-time budgets.

## Non-negotiable visual anchors

| Area | Approved direction |
|---|---|
| Heroine | Silver-white hair, violet eyes, white/gold/navy/violet armor, elegant sword silhouette, calm heroic posture |
| World | Large navigable valley with a river, bridges, village buildings, waterfalls, forest canopy, distant desert mesas, and snow mountains |
| Materials | Physically based stone, wet moss, wood grain, metal trim, cloth, ice, sand, and water foam; avoid flat debug colors in production assets |
| Lighting | Warm sunlight, cool mountain bounce, volumetric mist, soft firelight, readable silhouettes, cinematic depth without obscuring gameplay |
| Environment | Dense but authored foliage clusters, layered cliffs, paths, bridges, camps, farms, towers, and village construction pieces |
| UI presentation | Premium restrained HUD, readable mob HP bars, elite/boss accent colors, base markers, tool/building feedback, and clear resource-download states |

## Content-pack mapping

The approved direction maps to the existing downloadable pack taxonomy: `assetpack_characters` for hero and creature assets, `assetpack_forest` for the launch village and river, `assetpack_hd_textures` for PBR materials and decals, `assetpack_foliage_lods` for dense vegetation, `assetpack_terrain_lod` for cliffs and distant mountains, `assetpack_vfx` for mist/fire/water/weather effects, `assetpack_shaders_vulkan` and `assetpack_shaders_gles` for device-specific compiled materials, and `assetpack_world_streaming` for streamed sectors and navigation.

## Acceptance rule

New assets should be accepted only when they preserve the approved silhouette and palette, have a clear Unreal scale and collision setup, include a mobile LOD strategy, and contribute to the river-village-biome world rather than acting as unrelated decorative content. The visual target guides production; it does not claim that a concept image is itself a finished playable 3D asset.
