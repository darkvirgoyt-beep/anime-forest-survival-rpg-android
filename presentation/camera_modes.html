04 / Camera

One camera contract supports two play styles

A single mathematical foundation drives both traversal and immersive exploration.

Third-person

Follow camera positioned behind and above the visible player avatar. Optimized for traversal, spatial awareness, and observing the character's interaction with landmarks.

First-person

Eye-level camera with a foreground weapon element. The avatar body is hidden to prevent clipping. Optimized for close exploration and atmospheric immersion.

Shared State

Both modes consume the exact same yaw, pitch, target, projection, and movement-facing direction data. The engine simply applies a different offset vector.

Mobile Input

Left-side screen area handles locomotion. Right-side swipe orbit provides full horizontal 360° rotation and clamped vertical pitch, keeping controls consistent across views.
