06 / Multiplayer

The co-op tower room synchronizes the experience

A lightweight HTTPS rendezvous layer keeps friends aligned without a full authoritative simulation server.

Room Codes & Auth

A player creates a six-character room code; friends join through authenticated requests tied to their Google session.

Presence Heartbeats

Two-second heartbeats publish bounded position, active presence, and the latest tower-arrival revision.

Shared World Clock

The room service advances one shared clock and returns it, ensuring weather and day-night phases match for everyone.

Peer Rendering

Clients render remote friends as original low-poly companions and apply the shared world time to the local GLES3 scene.

Result  Players experience the same environment and see each other, setting the stage for shared events.
