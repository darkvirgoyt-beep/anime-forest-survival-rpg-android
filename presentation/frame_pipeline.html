02 / Runtime

The frame pipeline keeps simulation stable

Rendering speed can vary by device; the gameplay clock does not need to.
01

Android input

Touch, gyro, HUD actions, and a bounded frame delta enter through the native bridge.

02

Fixed step

An accumulator advances physics and gameplay in stable 60 Hz increments.

03

World update

Calendar, weather, combat, locomotion, tower cooldowns, and camera state update.

04

GLES3 frame

3D geometry renders first; the Android HUD remains a readable overlay above it.

Invariant  movement, weather motion, combat timing, and tower cooldowns are not tied to display refresh rate.
