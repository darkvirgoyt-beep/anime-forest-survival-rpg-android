03 / World Design

Procedural 3D composition prioritizes spatial clarity

Original terrain regions, landmarks, and procedural forms establish a readable environment without relying on external assets.

Environmental Bands

Large color fields divide the world into forest, sand, and snow regions, creating immediate navigation cues and distinct biomes.

Readable Silhouettes

Procedural terrain, trees, paths, and structures provide original, highly readable forms. The focus is on spatial understanding rather than photorealism.

The Teleportation Tower

An amber and dark-slate tower acts as the central anchor. It serves simultaneously as a fast-travel mechanic and a world-scale visual landmark.

Future-Proof Foundation

Authored meshes and high-fidelity materials can seamlessly replace these procedural primitives later without altering the underlying gameplay contracts.
