07 / Co-op Mechanics

Tower teleport is a readable shared event

A simple revision-based mechanic ensures all friends converge on the landmark simultaneously.

1. Local Activation

A player activates the tower, moving locally to the arrival point and incrementing their room revision.

2. Server Authority

The server records the highest accepted revision and exposes it to all current room members via heartbeats.

3. Remote Synchronization

Friends observe a newer remote revision and perform a synchronized arrival teleport to match the state.
