05 / Atmosphere

Weather and time turn the map into a living system

Ambient light, sky color, and procedural particles are driven by deterministic clocks rather than large texture libraries.
15-Minute World Clock
Day → Afternoon → Evening → Night
Drives the calendar, ambient brightness, and sky color. Evening shifts to warmer tones; night reduces light substantially and emphasizes the dark sky.
Independent Weather
Clear → Rain → Thunderstorm
Runs on its own repeating loop. Storms can cross day-night boundaries, adding procedural rain streaks and lightning flashes to the 3D scene.
Reproducibility  Deterministic effects ensure the same room state is reproducible across all Android clients without heavy network sync.
