05 / Production risk

THE RISK IS DEPTH — NOT MORE MENUS

The current prototype explains the intended genre. It does not yet prove the systems that make an RPG durable, threatening, persistent, or production-scalable.

■Core warningDo not expand placeholder surface area faster than combat authority, save truth, device performance, and authored 3D content.
Severity
Gap
Why it matters

2D presentation
Procedural GLES primitives cannot validate real 3D collision, traversal, animation, camera obstruction, or the visual target.
High
Static warden
No AI attack, telegraph, player damage, stagger, death loop, or positioning mastery means combat has little threat.
High
UI-only inventory
Equipment changes session UI state but does not yet drive authoritative combat stats, item instances, or durable state.
High
No profile save
Quest, XP, materials, inventory, and equipment cannot become a trustworthy RPG loop when they reset on initialization.
High
Hard-coded content
Embedded resource points, counters, enemy placement, and item rules make balancing and content expansion expensive.
Critical
No authority
Co-op, reconnect, replicated loot, building, anti-duplication, and cloud sync remain unvalidated without server authority.
The priority response is a device-tested Unreal forest slice plus authoritative inventory/save foundations — then content scale.
